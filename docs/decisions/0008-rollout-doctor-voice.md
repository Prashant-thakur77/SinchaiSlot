# 0008 Rollout calculator, Pump Doctor, browser voice, evaluator page

Each was checked for need before it was built.

| Feature | Who needs it, and why | For | Against | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rollout calculator with a site check | Operators and funders choosing sites. GIZ and KPMG (SWC 2023) found 1 of 51 owners of a multi-machine controller running anything besides the pump: no machine, pump far from home, single-phase motor. Our gain depends on those loads. | Answers the hardest question about the model; covers crops, places and unit economics | An estimate scaled from one simulated site | Built. The site check switches the loads off. Labelled an estimate |
| Pump Doctor | The Saur Mitra. Only about a third of pumps on a state portal still transmitted (CEEW). IWMI rates repair costs of individually owned pumps very high | Uses readings the drive already has, no new sensor | Simulated pumps; needs a neighbouring pump for panel faults; blind to some leaks | Built, with misses and false alarms published |
| Browser voice on the phone screen | Farmers who do not read; anyone judging the call | Free, no key, no audio sent to our server | Hindi voice reads Maithili and Bhojpuri; recognition may go to the browser maker | Built, opt-in, limits stated on screen |
| Evaluator page | People reviewing the project in a few minutes | One path through the evidence, with the limits | Not used by farmers | Built as plain links, no separate numbers |
| Evening water tank | Vegetable growers: one Chakhaji account says solar cannot serve evenings | Real need | A tank for one vegetable plot's evening is a civil-works cost we have no local price for | Not built; a pilot question |

## What the calculator showed
With a paid operator the owner earns less than an owner selling by the hour. The model works when the owner
or the group that owns the pump also runs it. The screen says so.

## Pump Doctor method
Design and alert levels from 2022-2023 weather, scored on 2024-2025. A first design that compared each pump
with the weather service found 61.9% of faults (39.3% right cause) and was replaced by comparison with
neighbouring pumps. Results: `reports/doctor.json`, regenerated by `python -m sinchai_sim.doctor_eval`.
